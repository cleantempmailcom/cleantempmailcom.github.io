Travel Deal Sites Asking for Your Email Before Showing Prices? Think First

You just want to see flight and hotel prices for your next trip, and a pop-up says "enter your email to unlock deals". Handing over your main address for casual price checking, then getting a stream of "limited-time offers", is a fair reason to hesitate. This guide helps you tell when a temporary inbox is fine and when you need your own address.

Why travel sites ask for emails so often

A common pattern: browsing is free, but member prices, price-drop alerts or saved trips need an email first. After that you often get promotions on top of the notifications you wanted.

If you are still comparing and have not decided to book, that address only needs to work for a short while.

02

When a temporary inbox fits

  • You only want to see member prices or deals and are not booking yet.
  • You want to try a price alert to see if the site is worth trusting.
  • You are comparing one destination across several sites and do not want to leave your main address on each.

In these cases the address only receives a confirmation or a few alerts, then you can walk away.

03

When to use your own email

  • Real bookings: confirmations, changes and refund notices for flights and hotels must stay findable, so use your main address.
  • Reservations you may need support for: emails in a temporary inbox are not stored permanently, so they are not proof of booking.
  • Loyalty and mileage accounts: these are long-term, and recovering one tied to a temporary address may be impossible.

A simple test: will you still need the mail this address receives in three months? If yes, use your own.

04

Steps with CleanTempMail

  1. Open CleanTempMail and generate a free temporary address, no signup; use a random prefix or a custom, memorable one.
  2. Copy it and paste it into the travel site.
  3. Return to the inbox, turn on auto refresh or refresh manually, and wait for the confirmation email.
  4. Open the email to read the body and click the confirmation link. External images are blocked, which reduces tracking.
  5. When you are done comparing, delete emails you no longer need or clear the inbox.

If the confirmation never shows up, see what to do when the email does not arrive.

05

Once you decide to book

When you have picked a trip, book with your main address and double-check its spelling on the order page. For more on shopping signups, read temporary email for online shopping; to grab a discount without marketing emails, see avoiding marketing emails when signing up for coupons.

FAQ

Can I book flights or hotels with a temporary email?

Not recommended. Confirmations, changes and refund notices need to be kept, so use your main address.

Do I need to register for the inbox?

No. CleanTempMail is free and you can generate an address right on the page.

Are emails kept forever?

No. Emails are temporary and not stored permanently, so save anything you need.

Can I choose my own prefix?

Yes, or use a random one.

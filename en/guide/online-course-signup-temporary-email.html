Free Online Course Wants Your Email? Think Before You Sign Up

You find a free online course, click through, and must create an account with your email first. You only want to sample a lesson or two, yet course recommendations and promotions may follow for months. This guide shows how to protect your main address while sampling, and when to switch to a permanent one.

Why course platforms ask for email first

Course platforms use email to open your account and send class notices, and often to promote other courses. While sampling, you have not decided to keep learning, and handing over your main address makes the promotions hard to shake off later.

The idea matches trying free software: sample with a separate address, then move to your usual one if the course is worth it.

02

Four checks before enrolling

  • Is signup required to preview: some course pages show the syllabus or a sample clip openly.
  • Tick boxes: untick pre-selected marketing options before submitting.
  • Certificates or payment: if you want a completion certificate or a paid course, use a permanent address.
  • Study length: if you plan to study long term and keep your records, use a permanent address too.
03

Five steps to sample a course with a temporary email

  1. Open a temporary inbox: the CleanTempMail web inbox generates an address free with no signup, random or with a custom prefix.
  2. Enter it on the enrollment page and finish signing up.
  3. Collect the confirmation email: refresh manually or automatically, open the message, then click the link or copy the code.
  4. Sample and decide: if you keep going, change the account email to your usual one in settings so a lapsed temporary address does not lock you out.
  5. Walk away cleanly: temporary emails are not stored permanently; delete or clear what you do not need.
04

Courses that need a permanent address

  • Courses where you want a certificate, credits or proof of completion.
  • Paid courses, where you may need receipts or support by email.
  • Accounts you plan to keep for long-term study and progress.

See temporary vs permanent email for how to choose.

05

If the confirmation email never arrives

Check the address for typos, refresh the inbox manually, wait a minute or two, and use the page's resend option if needed. More steps in what to do when email does not arrive.

FAQ

Can I enroll in an online course with a temporary email?

While sampling, yes. For certificates, paid courses or long-term study, use a permanent address.

What if I want to keep learning?

Change the account email to your usual one in settings before the temporary address lapses.

Do I need to sign up for CleanTempMail?

No. The web inbox is free and needs no signup.

Are emails kept forever?

No. Emails are temporary and not stored permanently; save anything important.

Can I choose a custom prefix?

Yes, the web inbox supports random addresses and custom prefixes.

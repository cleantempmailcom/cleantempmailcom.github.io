Need just one verification code? A five-step signup without your real email

Download a PDF, try a small tool, join a guest Wi-Fi: the site wants your email, and promotional mail may follow for months. If you only need one code or one confirmation message, these five steps keep your everyday inbox out of it.

First, decide whether the signup deserves your real email

A simple test: will you ever need to recover this account? For a quick look at content, a one-off download or a few minutes with a small tool, a temporary inbox is enough. For payments, orders, ID, work or data you want to keep, use an email address you control long term. A fuller comparison is in temporary vs permanent email.

02

Five steps to sign up

  1. Open an inbox. Go to the CleanTempMail web app. No account is needed and you get a random address; you can also choose a custom prefix.
  2. Copy the address. Paste it into the site's email field. If the site rejects a domain, try another available one.
  3. Keep the page open. After submitting, return to the inbox and wait for auto-refresh, or refresh manually.
  4. Read the message. Open it, copy the code or click the confirmation link. The body and attachments can be viewed in the inbox.
  5. Clean up. Delete the message or clear the inbox so codes and links do not linger at a reachable address.

If nothing arrives after a while, work through the not-received checklist.

04

What it keeps away from you

Marketing mail goes to an address you can discard, not your daily inbox. CleanTempMail's tracking protection blocks external images in messages, reducing the chance of tracking pixels reporting back when you open one. More checks are in the pre-signup privacy checklist.

Keep in mind

The official privacy and security notes say emails are temporary and not stored permanently, so save anything you need. Also follow the target site's rules on temporary email.

05

Three small habits

  • Use one address per site so you can clear them separately.
  • To return to the same inbox later, save its direct-inbox link; it works like a key, so do not share it casually.
  • For passwords, use the built-in strong password generator. It does not store your account, so record it yourself.
06

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to give my real email to sign up?

Not always. For one-off verification or a download, a temporary inbox is enough; use a long-term address for important accounts.

Do I need an account to use a temporary inbox?

No. The CleanTempMail web inbox is free and needs no signup.

The site says my email is not supported. What now?

Try another available domain. If it still fails, the site restricts it; respect that and use a long-term address.

The code never arrives. What should I check?

Check the spelling and refresh the inbox, then follow the not-received guide step by step.

Are messages kept forever?

No. The official notes say emails are temporary and not stored permanently, so save what matters.
